Editorial Note

The Continuum: An Academic Journal is an annual, peer-reviewed scholarly journal. The First Volume is dedicated to advancing critical inquiry and interdisciplinary research across the humanities, social sciences, and allied disciplines. The journal provides a platform for original research articles, review essays, and critical perspectives that engage with contemporary academic and societal concerns.

Committed to academic rigour, ethical publishing practices, and intellectual diversity, the Continuum: An Academic Journal encourages contributions from established scholars and doctoral students. All submissions undergo a rigorous peer-review process to ensure quality and relevance.

The journal welcomes contributions from a wide range of fields, including—but not limited to—humanities, social sciences, computer science, engineering, management studies, agricultural sciences, and medical and health sciences, fostering dialogue across disciplines and contributing meaningfully to national and international scholarly conversations.
